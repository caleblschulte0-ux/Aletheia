Only your keyboard can grant these

Two permissions are deliberately not buttons: standing authority over routine work, and permission to answer a conversation in your name. Each one widens what she may do without asking, and a grant that can be given by tapping is a grant something else can give. Type them here, on this PC:

python -m aletheia.standing on

python -m aletheia.conversations grant "<your own words>"

To see what she may already do without asking, ask her: what can you do without asking me?
